# BSC Island: master vision

A Roblox experience for the Kuwait University College of Science **Biological Sciences Club (BSC)**. It is fun
first, with light and accurate science, and it should feel like one experience, not just a parkour map.

## Pillars
1. **Campus World:** the College of Science, built as close to reality as we can.
   - Exterior: real and recognisable. Every detail is graded A (documented), B (inferred) or C (game addition).
   - Interior: does not have to match reality, but must be beautiful, spacious, easy to navigate and calm.
   - Contains the BSC HQ, the social spaces and (later) the Biology Discovery Museum.
2. **Science Adventure:** the island hub, the 0–100 parkour, the Specialties Train and the Cell Train.
3. **BSC Arcade:** real competitive and co-op games, not empty rooms and not only quiz questions.
4. **Biology Labs:** interactive simulations with correct science, Arabic + English terms, and an honest simulation
   label. One complete lab at a time, starting with Light Microscopy.
5. **Game Show:** a club-event quiz system with varied round formats.
6. **Special & Community:** stages made by friends (with review) and future content.

## Rules
- All worlds are open from the start. Progress is cosmetic: Passport, XP, titles, badges.
- No pay-to-win. Important rewards are verified on the server.
- Never publish or merge without the owner's approval. Never delete existing systems without approval.
- Only licensed or permitted audio and images.
- Deferred: the horror / bio-containment world. It stays in the roadmap only.
- Ship one complete feature at a time. No fake buttons or placeholder systems.
