# Micro Racers v47 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans inline. Fresh independent whole-branch review before delivery.

**Goal:** Two fully drivable three-lap micro-themed tracks, solo AI and real multiplayer, keyboard and phone.

**Architecture:** Add a standalone race entrance within Arcade, outside its random-round loop. Race lobby/track geometry and vehicle templates are authored in Python. A pure shared simulation owns steering, acceleration, braking, road constraints, sequential checkpoints and ranks. Server simulates all vehicles from clamped inputs, creates immutable race IDs and records only verified finishes. Clients render interpolated local clones of an authored vehicle and control a chase camera; players stay in the race lobby until exit. No client positions/laps/rewards are accepted.

**Tech Stack:** Luau, Python, Rojo, Lune, Studio MCP, VirtualInput, StudioTestService.

**Spec:** Supervisor accepted v46 commit73d3b4c and selected Micro Racers. Two distinct layouts (easy and technical with fair shortcut), three laps, checkpoint order/rank/results/replay/track choice, temporary boost/shield/light obstacle, actual manual steering and throttle/brake, recovery, solo AI finishers and actual multiplayer. Explicit death/respawn/exit/disconnect and exploit tests. Owner authorized continuing and routine implementation choices.

## Review Focus

- Out-of-order gates, forged positions/laps/rewards, NaN/infinite input, stale race IDs and duplicate finish must be rejected.
- Death, respawn, travel, departure, timeout and disconnect must release anchoring and controls without affecting another racer.
- Race inputs stop on missing packets/focus loss; no automatic driving for human players.
- AI and human use the same road/progress physics; shortcuts do not skip required gates.
- Results/queue/replay state cannot attach an old participant to a new race; phone targets avoid CoreGui and camera cleans up.
