## PART C — REFERENCE RESEARCH & DESIGN PRINCIPLES ADOPTED

Per Section 3 of the Directive, we studied benchmark Roblox experiences across each target genre and extracted concrete engineering and level-design principles:

1. **Cart Rides & Rail Attractions (`Cart Ride Around Nothing`, `Theme Park Tycoon 2`)**:
   - *Principles Adopted*: Physical on-cart 3D dashboard buttons (`ClickDetector`s on Start/Stop, Throttle +/-, Horn) paired with automatic curve speed-limiting (`v^2 / R <= LATERAL`) and smooth exponential rotation damping (`1 - math.exp(-dt * 10)`) so carts never jitter or unfairly derail on curves.
2. **Educational & Science Rides (`Cell Train` / Dark-Ride Attractions)**:
   - *Principles Adopted*: Never lock the player into a fixed scriptable camera that fights mouse/touch input; default to `Enum.CameraType.Custom` seated free-look so riders can look 360° around the cell membrane, nucleus, mitochondria, and Golgi apparatus, while auto-slowing (`SLOW = 7`) near landmark stops.
3. **Obstacle Courses & Puzzle Obbies (`Tower of Hell`, `Flood Escape 2`)**:
   - *Principles Adopted*: Zero non-gameplay collision on floating UI/signage (`CanCollide = false`, `CanQuery = false` on all instruction boards and button labels); idempotent tile activation (stepping on an already-activated green sequence tile must *never* penalize or reset the player); generous tile spacing (`8.4` studs center-to-center) so camera angles and jump arcs never brush adjacent tiles.
4. **Infection / Tag Modes (`Infectious Smile`, `Evade`)**:
   - *Principles Adopted*: History-aware role selection (`Random.new()` with `lastZero` deprioritization so the same player is never picked as Patient Zero twice in a row), clear silhouette highlights (`Highlight` instance with distinct role colors), vaccine shield pickups, and a solo practice bot/drone mode when only 1 player is in the arena.
5. **Multiplayer Trivia / Elimination Stages (`TriviaShow`, `Blockate Quiz`)**:
   - *Principles Adopted*: Lock-in buzzer (`2.0s` before floor drop) that records `committed[player] = zoneOf(player)` so players cannot straddle zone borders or jump at `t = 0.0s` to survive without knowing the answer.
