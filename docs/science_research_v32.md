## Confirmed and built
- South facade: faceted bronze perforated screen (geometric cut-outs, folded corners, sloped crown) over a colonnade;
  a central bay of tall white columns in front of glazing with horizontal louvres; a long, low massing.
- Plaza: light limestone, palm row along the road, stone planters, the "114" stone; warm pavers under the colonnade.
- North entrance: glass curtain wall with vertical metal fins and a low limestone "College of Science" wall.
- Interiors: white marble, steel-clad round columns, skylit atrium (already present), colourful study lounge
  (green/blue/orange sofas, red high-back booths, cube poufs), red auditorium seats with wood back wall.

## Approximate (not confirmed in detail)
- Faculty club: the stacked curved white balconies and roof garden are confirmed; its exact plan shape (elongated in

- Scale: about 1:2 of the real building; the side wings stand for the rest of the long south facade (exterior only).
